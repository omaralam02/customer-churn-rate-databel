# Customer Churn Rate of Databel

## Dataset
[Customer Churn Rate - Databel](https://github.com/omaralam02/airbnb-property-roi/blob/main/Airbnb%20Property%20ROI.xlsx)

## Project Objective
Databel wants to know about the churn rate of its customers and the causes behind those churn rate.

## Process
• Cleaned raw dataset and performed EDA to uncover corelations impacting customer churn rate.
• Created pivot tables to summarize insights by customer segments, contract types and others.
• Built dynamic dashboards and visualizations to effectively communicate findings to non-technical stakeholders. 

## Dashboard and Charts
![Customer Churn Rate Dashboard](https://github.com/omaralam02/customer-churn-rate-databel/blob/main/Churn%20Rate%20Dashboard.png)

## Project Insights
• Customer Churn Rate is 26.86%                 
• Two of the main reasons for churn are competitor had better devices and made better offers.    
• Senior customers who are above the age of 65 make up to 44% of the total churned customers.     
• CA state has the highest and RI state has the lowest percentage of churn rate.
